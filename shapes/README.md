# Shapes

A **shape** is the fixed skeleton of one kind of document: every document of that kind has the same parts in the same order. The shapes particular to a blueprint are listed here. The general ones, and the topic every folder follows, are in [reader-ergonomics](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics/blob/65e42472e2d4cbfd994c2a86e95a47e5dc7301ca/shapes/README.md).

## Contents

| Shape | For |
|---|---|
| [claim.md](claim.md) | one claim of a proof: a sentence, its Lean declaration, a reading in words, two audit lines, the statement and the proof |

## Choosing a shape

- One document, one shape: a theorem is a topic that lists its claims, and each claim follows this shape.
- Each part of a shape says where it comes from, with the grade of its evidence.
