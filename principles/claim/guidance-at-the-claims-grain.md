# Guidance at the claim's grain

**Claim:** a verdict, an error or a hint names the person's claim, not the checker's internal step.

- **Grade:** supported with caveat: messages at the checker's grain stopped students; convention, untested for the grain itself.
- **Evidence:** [Guo 2026b](../../evidence/bibliography.md), sections 7.2.2 and 8.3.1; [Carl 2022](../../evidence/bibliography.md), section 5; [position, condition 3](../../evidence/autoformalization/position.md).
- **Check:** `lean-link` (exact): a failing declaration maps to one claim topic; `topic` (exact): each claim is listed by its parent; whether a message helps the person is judgment, measured on what the person does next.
- **Reader styles:** none.

## What it asks

- Report each failure against the claim topic that names the declaration.
- Give the claim's title and link, not only the checker's goal.

## Why

- A message in the checker's words sends the person to a place they did not write.
- One declaration per claim lets a program turn the checker's name into the person's claim.

## Limits

- No study measured whether a proof assistant's messages help any user.
- Guidance from the loop is not built yet; this rule fixes where it will speak.
