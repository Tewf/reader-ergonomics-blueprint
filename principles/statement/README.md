# Statement: what the person audits

The Lean statement of a claim is where translation fails, and Lean cannot say whether it means what its source means. These rules keep it short, apart from its proof, and tied to one claim. The shape is the [claim](../../shapes/claim.md).

| Principle | Claim |
|---|---|
| [A short statement, apart from its proof](short-statement-apart-from-proof.md) | the statement a person audits sits in a short file of its own, and each proof is held to it |
| [One claim, one declaration](one-claim-one-declaration.md) | a claim names exactly one Lean declaration, and no declaration serves two claims |
| [The model decides nothing](the-model-decides-nothing.md) | the person audits the statement that was checked, and a model's reading of it is labelled as one |
